About Mazakha Foundation

Mazakha Foundation supports young people through youth empowerment, the We Are Human education programme, and community development initiatives that strengthen families and neighbourhoods.

See programs → Our stories
who we are

Planting the roots of tomorrow’s leaders

Founded to expand access to learning and leadership opportunities, Mazakha Foundation partners with local leaders, schools and donors to deliver mentorship, scholarships and community-led projects. We prioritise dignity, practical skills and long-term relationships that allow young people to thrive.

Our approach

  • Youth empowerment: One-on-one mentorship and leadership development.
  • We Are Human: Education supports, scholarships and after-school learning.
  • Community development: Local projects led by young people that build skills and safe spaces.

Get involved

Support our work by donating, volunteering as a mentor, or partnering on a local project. Every contribution helps create opportunities for youth to learn, lead and build stronger communities across South Africa.
