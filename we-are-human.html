our programs

Education & Learner Support

We believe that every child deserves the opportunity to attend school with dignity and the resources they need to succeed. Our education initiatives provide practical support to learners and schools, including school shoe donations, educational prizes and support for academic achievement. We also use our programmes to expose young people to new opportunities, including communication, media and mentorship experiences. By reducing some of the financial barriers faced by vulnerable families, we help learners remain engaged in education and focused on their future.

Our Commitment

To restore dignity, create opportunity and empower communities through education, sport, sustainable development and welfare initiatives.
